What is MUN?

Model United Nations (MUN) is an academic simulation of the United Nations where students take on the roles of delegates and engage in debates, negotiations, and diplomacy. It helps participants develop public speaking, research, leadership, and teamwork skills while gaining deeper insights into international relations and global issues.

At BTAL MUN, students experience a dynamic and respectful environment to discuss world affairs and become the change-makers of tomorrow.

Benefits of Participating

BTALIMUN offers a unique opportunity to enhance leadership, diplomacy, and critical thinking skills while connecting with peers worldwide.
